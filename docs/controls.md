# Controllers and button mappings

Use **Launcher → Settings → Controls** before playing, or **F6** in game. Guitar and gamepad profiles are saved separately.

## Choose a device

Select XInput or DirectInput as appropriate for your controller, then choose it by name. Automatic selection uses the available controller. Saved DirectInput devices can be recognized after reconnecting; XInput selection follows the Windows controller slot.

## Choose a layout

- **Native GH2 controls:** use the game's normal layout. On a standard gamepad, frets are green LT, red LB, yellow RB, blue RT and orange A. Notes are automatically strummed in this layout.
- **Custom bindings:** choose buttons for frets, strumming, Star Power and Pause.
- **Custom guitar with strum:** use a gamepad as a guitar, with separate fret and strum inputs.

In the launcher, click a mapping and press the desired controller button. Release held inputs first. Escape cancels and right-click clears a mapping. Capture times out after 15 seconds without changing the previous binding.

Pause before remapping during a song. Mappings also affect navigation: guitar green confirms and red cancels. Use **Save** to keep profiles and device selection, or **Restore native profile** to reset the selected profile.

## DirectInput setup

Select a DirectInput device and open its device setup. Assign raw buttons, stick axes, triggers and D-pad directions. Button 0 means unbound; buttons 1–128 refer to the physical device's buttons. Raw button mappings also support click-to-map capture.

The raw DirectInput layout is shared between DirectInput devices. Configure one device at a time. Adjust trigger direction and rest position if an action stays active when the controller is idle.

## Whammy and keyboard

Choose a whammy axis if your controller needs one, or keep native axes for an already working guitar. Star Power can use a button or a supported tilt/stick input.

Keyboard bindings are available under **F4 → Input/Keybinds**. These inputs follow the selected controller profile. The input monitor in F6 can help identify buttons and axes.
